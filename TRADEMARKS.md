anyone who sees "CIRKS" knows what they are getting and who stands behind it.

## You may, without asking

- **Say that you use CIRKS.** For example: "We manage information risk with
  CIRKS."
- **Redistribute CIRKS unmodified** under its own name, with the `LICENSE` and
  `NOTICE` files intact.
- **Describe your own work truthfully.** For example: "based on CIRKS", "a fork
  of CIRKS", "compatible with CIRKS", "imports data from CIRKS". Keep the name
  in plain text, without the CIRKS logo, and do not suggest that Wightman Brown
  Limited made, endorses or supports your work.
- **Write, teach and talk about CIRKS**: articles, reviews, conference talks,
  and internal training for your own organisation.

## You need written permission to

- **Name a modified version CIRKS**, or anything confusingly similar, such as
  "CIRKS Pro" or "OpenCIRKS". A modified version must have a different name.
- **Offer services under the name**, such as "CIRKS training", "CIRKS
  consultancy", "CIRKS implementation", or a hosted or managed "CIRKS"
  service. You may provide those services; you may not sell them as CIRKS or
  imply they are official.
- **Offer "CIRKS certification"**, or call anyone a certified CIRKS
  practitioner.
- **Use the name in a company name, product name, domain name or social media
  account.**
- **Use the CIRKS name or logo on merchandise**, or in advertising for anything
  other than CIRKS itself.

## Always

- Keep the copyright, licence and trademark notices that come with the software:
